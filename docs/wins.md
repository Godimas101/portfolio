# Wins

**Career moments worth remembering.** 15+ years across mobile, social, indie, AAA console, live service, and now AI-assisted QA.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-notebook-heart:{ .lg .middle } **Captain's Log**

    ---

    *Prodigy Education · June–July 2026 · 5 weeks*

    5-week solo build with an AI collaborator from empty repo to **Prodigy HR adopting it company-wide** for performance-review prep. Tauri 2 + Rust + Svelte macOS tray app. ~47,000 lines of application source, 554 automated tests, 21 shipped phases.

    The design decision that won HR: the app does not write your performance review. It assembles source material and instructs the receiving LLM to produce point-form suggestions with week numbers and Jira refs — never draft answers. The human still writes the review, which is the entire point.

-   :material-transit-connection-variant:{ .lg .middle } **TestRail → AIO Tests migration**

    ---

    *Prodigy Education · Central QA · 2025–2026*

    Selected as one of only 2 QAers on the elite Central QA team. Led the company-wide test-management tool migration end-to-end — analysis, implementation, org rollout, training. Supported multiple game squads with Jira / Confluence workflow overhauls. Pioneered AI tooling in QA workflow (the seed of what became `prodigy-qa-skills`).

-   :material-earth-arrow-right:{ .lg .middle } **RPG localization launch**

    ---

    *Prodigy Education · 2025–2026 · 5 languages*

    End-to-end QA leadership on Prodigy RPG localization. Spanish and Portuguese shipped to production. Japanese, German, and French work completed and ready — currently unreleased pending business decisions. Managed 3 outsource testers and a 50-freelancer TestIO team through release. Test plans, bug triage, cross-department stakeholders, initial public release planning.

-   :material-source-branch-check:{ .lg .middle } **Prodigy's second game — workflow blueprint**

    ---

    *Prodigy Education · April 2021 – November 2024*

    Applied to and got onto Prodigy's second-ever game. Given a fresh start free of standard company practices, I built an automation-first team workflow that later became the model other Prodigy teams adopted: ticket templates, auto-assembled bug-triage lists + scheduled triage meetings, deep git integration moving tickets across boards on PR status, and the patch-notes bot all originated here.

    Led QA on multiple features including the multiplayer system. Also stood up a Jira help desk as a side project that grew into a company-wide function — now just two of us admin it, with more onboarding planned.

-   :material-swap-horizontal-bold:{ .lg .middle } **Phaser → Pixi engine migration**

    ---

    *Prodigy Education · RPG · 2020–2024*

    Inherited a struggling engine-conversion project from another team and led it to the finish line. Reorganized and coordinated 6 internal testers through to completion. Built the workflow pipeline for the developers and the project-management system for status tracking. Turned a stuck project into an organized delivery.

-   :material-account-multiple-plus:{ .lg .middle } **Producer on three teams at once**

    ---

    *Big Viking Games · London, ON · 2015–2016*

    Associate Producer directing three development teams (12+ people total) across two cities, sharing a single designer. Established the process foundations the growing studio ran on for years — Atlassian + Google-centric workflow standards for agile delivery on HTML5 games.

-   :material-hammer-screwdriver:{ .lg .middle } **Introduced Jira to Big Viking**

    ---

    *Big Viking Games · London, ON · 2013*

    Brought Jira + DevSuite into the studio as QA lead. Stood up multiple teams on Atlassian tools. Established company-wide QA standards for bug creation and build organization. Set the workflow foundation the studio scaled on.

-   :material-controller-classic:{ .lg .middle } **Splinter Cell: Blacklist**

    ---

    *Ubisoft Toronto · 2012–2013 · Metacritic 82–84*

    My first AAA release. Multiplayer testing (COOP + PVP) on a high-profile Tom Clancy title. Consistently top bug producer on the multiplayer team while maintaining ticket quality. Collaborated with international QA teams daily. Delivered clear, concise, complete bug documentation under demanding deadlines.

-   :material-school:{ .lg .middle } **Mentorship record**

    ---

    *Across Big Viking + Prodigy*

    ~7 QA hires onboarded over the two studios. Mentored a contractor QAer through full-time conversion → promotion. Trained an intern up to intermediate — they're now a Sr. QAer at a mining company. Trained the current 7-person Prodigy QA department on Claude Code skill use + AI/automation integration into daily QA workflows.
